# Yuh Blockin' — 30-Second Ad: Storyboard

Format: mixed — real app-screen recordings intercut with real-world/lifestyle
shots. Runtime: 30s. Designed to work muted (every VO line is mirrored as
on-screen text).

Raw app-screen footage already captured this session, stored outside the repo
at `~/Videos/yuh_blockin_ad_footage/` (not committed — binary video doesn't
belong in git):
- `send_flow.mp4` — 720×1280, ~95s raw
- `receive_flow.mp4` — 720×1600, ~30s raw

Both are raw/uncut takes with natural pauses between actions — an editor
should trim each down to the few seconds actually needed per beat below, not
use them full-length.

---

### 0:00–0:04 — THE PROBLEM
**Visual (real-world — not yet sourced):** Exterior, daytime. A driver walks
up to their car, boxed in tight by another car parked across the
driveway/spot. A beat of visible frustration — checking their phone, looking
around for the owner.
**On-screen text:** *"Blocked in. Again."*
**VO / copy:** *"You're blocked in — and you have no idea whose car this is."*
**SFX:** Ambient street noise, a soft frustrated sigh.

### 0:04–0:09 — THE OLD WAY (quick, almost comedic)
**Visual (real-world — not yet sourced, fast cuts):** Quick 1-second flashes —
someone awkwardly asking a stranger, someone leaving a passive-aggressive
paper note under a wiper, someone just waiting by their car.
**On-screen text:** *"Notes. Guesswork. Awkward confrontations."*
**SFX:** A record-scratch or comedic sting on the note-under-wiper shot.

### 0:09–0:19 — THE SOLUTION (app screen recording)
**Visual — footage ready:** `send_flow.mp4`. Shows: home screen → tap hero
"I'm Blocked" button → bottom sheet → inline panel (plate entry, emoji/urgency
selector) → tap Send → confirmation (Activity feed updates).
**On-screen text (sync to taps):** *"Scan the plate."* → *"Send the alert."*
→ *"They're notified instantly."*
**VO / copy:** *"Yuh Blockin' sends a silent, instant alert straight to their
phone — no confrontation, no personal info exchanged."*
**SFX:** Light, modern UI tap sounds; a soft notification chime on the
"instantly" beat.

### 0:19–0:25 — TRUST / SAFETY (app screen recording, brief)
**Visual — footage ready:** `receive_flow.mp4`. Shows the received alert in
Alert History → the Hide / Report / Block sender menu open on the alert.
**On-screen text:** *"You're always in control. Block or report anyone,
anytime."*
**VO / copy:** *"Built with safety first — you decide who can reach you."*

### 0:25–0:30 — CTA / LOGO LOCKUP
**Visual (asset, not footage — app icon + store badges, easy to produce
directly, no filming needed):** App icon animates in on a clean background
(white or brand color), App Store + Google Play badges fade in beneath it.
**On-screen text:** *"Yuh Blockin' — Get unblocked."*
**VO / copy:** *"Download Yuh Blockin' today."*
**SFX:** Short, warm musical resolve/stinger.

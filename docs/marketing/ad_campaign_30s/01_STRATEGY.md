# Yuh Blockin' — 30-Second Ad: Strategy

## Objective
Give Yuh Blockin' a short, general-purpose promo piece (not tied to a single ad
platform) usable on the website, in social posts, or as a store listing preview.
Primary goal: make a stranger who has never heard of the app understand, in 30
seconds, exactly what problem it solves and why it's safe to use.

## The problem we're selling the solution to
Getting blocked in by another car is a universally annoying, low-stakes-but-
irritating moment. Today people deal with it by guessing, leaving notes, or
awkward face-to-face confrontation. Yuh Blockin' replaces all of that with one
tap.

## Core message
**"Get unblocked without the confrontation."**
One tap sends a silent, instant alert to the car that's blocking you — no
personal information exchanged, no awkward conversation, no note on a
windshield.

## Secondary message (trust/safety)
This app handles a scenario that could easily feel confrontational or unsafe,
so the ad explicitly earns trust: anonymity is real (no personal info
exchanged), and the user is always in control (block/report anyone, anytime).
This isn't an afterthought — it's a deliberate beat in the script, not just a
disclaimer.

## Target audience
Anyone who drives or parks in dense areas — apartment complexes, city streets,
shared driveways, double-parked delivery zones. Broad, not a narrow niche.
Tone should be relatable/everyday, not techy or salesy.

## Tone & style
Light, a little wry about the "old way" (notes, awkward confrontation), then
confident and clean once the app is introduced. Modern, minimal, upbeat —
matches the app's own visual language (clean blue/white UI, the "Move with
respect." tagline).

## Format
- 30 seconds, HD.
- Mixed footage: real captured app-screen recordings (authentic, store-safe —
  no mockups) intercut with real-world/lifestyle shots (driveway scene, note-
  under-wiper gag, etc.).
- Works muted (all key beats are also on-screen text) since a lot of first
  exposure on social/web autoplay happens without sound.

## Call to action
"Download Yuh Blockin' today" + app icon / store badges on screen for the
final beat. No pricing or urgency language — this is an awareness piece, not a
conversion/retargeting ad.

## Notes on brand consistency
Reuse the app's own copy voice where possible — the in-app tagline "Move with
respect." and the zero-tolerance safety language already shipped in the app
(report/block, Terms of Service) should inform the safety beat's wording so
the ad doesn't contradict or duplicate messaging inconsistently.

# YuhBlockin Advertising Quality Control

A video is not finished merely because it looks impressive.

It must also:

- communicate the story
- communicate the product
- maintain brand accuracy
- maintain visual consistency
- contain technically clean footage
- have a clear CTA

## Reject

- malformed vehicles
- broken wheels
- obvious AI artifacts
- unwanted humans
- incorrect YuhBlockin UI
- incorrect logo
- invented claims
- unreadable critical text
- accidental third-party branding
- inconsistent primary vehicle

## Review

Check:

1. Brand
2. Story
3. Product
4. Visual quality
5. Caribbean authenticity
6. Animation
7. Continuity
8. UI accuracy
9. Audio
10. CTA
11. Platform suitability

Use:

PASS
NEEDS REVISION
FAIL

# YuhBlockin Environments

When targeting BVI users, use believable BVI/Caribbean environments.

Possible environments include:

- island roads
- commercial areas
- residential roads
- parking areas
- coastal roads
- marinas
- ferry areas
- tropical roadside environments

Prioritize believable geography, architecture, lighting and road conditions.

Do not falsely identify a generic tropical environment as a specific BVI landmark.

Maintain environmental continuity between shots.

# YuhBlockin Advertising Formats

## Default

9:16 vertical.

Initial target:

15–20 seconds.

## Structure

0–3 seconds:
HOOK

3–8 seconds:
PROBLEM

8–14 seconds:
YUHBLOCKIN ACTION

14–18 seconds:
RESOLUTION

18–20 seconds:
CTA

Adjust timing when the story requires it.

## Important

Critical text, UI and logos should be added during post-production.

Do not depend on generated video for exact text.

## Campaign Objective

Build first awareness of YuhBlockin in its launch market and communicate,
in one clear watch, what the app actually does and why it's safe to use.
This is an awareness/download-driver campaign, not a retention or
re-engagement campaign — there is no existing user base to speak to yet.

## Target Audience

Verified personas (source-of-truth §5), BVI-first: daily commuters/office
workers, taxi drivers, delivery drivers, shop staff, tourists & rental-vehicle
drivers. These personas share one thing in common — they park or are parked
around frequently, in tight/shared spaces, as part of a daily routine — which
is why a single flagship concept (see "Recommended Scope" below) can speak to
all of them without needing persona-specific cuts yet.

## Core Problem

Being blocked in by another vehicle with no way to reach the driver except
guesswork, waiting, or an awkward confrontation. This is the literal problem
named in the existing approved flyer copy ("Find the driver") and is a daily,
low-stakes-but-universally-irritating moment for anyone who parks in dense
BVI areas — not a hypothetical pain point.

## Product Mechanism Being Demonstrated

The real, verified "I'm Blocked" flow (source-of-truth §1, confirmed by live
testing and real footage this session):
tap the app → enter the blocking vehicle's plate → send → the other driver is
notified anonymously, with no names exchanged.

This is the one mechanism this campaign should demonstrate. It should be
shown accurately — the real UI, via compositing (see Product Authenticity
below) — not reinterpreted or simplified into something the app doesn't
actually do.

## Emotional Hook

Frustration → relief, without ever escalating to confrontation. The hook is
the moment of being stuck with no good options, resolved by one calm,
anonymous tap. This is also where "Move with respect" earns its meaning: the
resolution is quiet and respectful, not a "gotcha" or a callout.

## Visual World

Per the locked creative direction (**revised 2026-09-30: 2D flat/vector
animated with subtle depth, not 3D** — see
`docs/marketing/YUHBLOCKIN_BRAND_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` §12), Caribbean/BVI
environment — island roads, parking areas, coastal/marina or commercial
settings, tropical vegetation, natural island light. Palette: YuhBlockin logo
teal (`#0D7493`) and coral (`#FF7670`) as the primary brand accents, set
against natural Caribbean environmental color (ocean, greenery, sky,
sunlight/sunset), per the locked palette decision. Not a tourism-ad look —
believable, everyday BVI geography, not landmark showcasing.

## Vehicle-Character Direction

Two vehicles carry the story: the one that's blocked in, and the one that's
blocking it. Neither is anthropomorphized (no faces, no dialogue, no
cartoon eyes) — personality comes through movement, positioning, headlights/
indicators, and timing, per the Ad Studio's vehicle-universe rules. The
"resolution" beat is the blocking vehicle moving — that's the entire payoff,
and it should read clearly without needing a human on screen to react to it.

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. **Hook** — the relatable moment of being blocked in, no visible way out
2. **"Move with respect. Find the driver. Clear the road."** (existing
   approved tagline/positioning — carries the core message)
3. **Product beat** — real app UI, composited in: tap → plate → send →
   notified
4. **"No names. No confrontation. Just movement."** (trust/differentiator,
   directly supports the anonymity claim that's actually true per §1/§6)
5. **CTA** — logo lockup + download prompt

## Desired Viewer Action

Download YuhBlockin. No specific store link or promo code is verified as
current for this campaign brief (the flyer's "Scan to Download
(TestFlight/App Store)" is dated 2025 copy and TestFlight is no longer the
live distribution channel — see source-of-truth §7/§10) — the CTA should stay
generic ("Download YuhBlockin") unless a current store link is confirmed
before the EDIT phase.

## Recommended Scope for This Campaign

**One flagship concept**, not multiple persona variants, for this first
campaign. Reasoning:
- The core problem and mechanism are identical across every listed persona —
  a single well-told "blocked in" scenario is broadly relatable without
  needing separate cuts
- This is the first time the locked visual system (palette, 2D flat/vector
  style, UI compositing pipeline) will be produced — validating it once,
  well, is lower risk than generating multiple concepts against an unproven
  pipeline
- Matches the Ad Studio's own operating principle: consistency over novelty,
  one primary problem per ad

If the flagship concept lands well, natural next-phase variants (not part of
this campaign) would be persona-specific cuts — e.g. a taxi/delivery-driver
version, a tourist/rental-car version — reusing the same visual system and
messaging hierarchy with a different opening scenario.

Private driver notification for the British Virgin Islands

A simple way to let someone know they're blocking your vehicle. No phone numbers. No confrontation. Just a quiet notification.

The situation

Parking in the BVI is tight. Double-parking happens. It's rarely malicious—just the reality of limited space.

The usual options—honking, waiting, or tracking someone down—don't respect anyone's time. They create friction where none is needed.

YuhBlockin' offers a calmer alternative.

How it works

Step 1

Register

Sign up and receive a unique vehicle code. Display it on your dashboard.

Step 2

Notify

If blocked, enter the other vehicle's code. They receive a private notification.

Step 3

Resolve

They move their car. You're on your way. No drama, no confrontation.

Privacy and data

We collect only what's necessary: a device token for notifications and a hashed vehicle identifier. Your actual plate number is converted to a secure hash that even we cannot reverse.

Notifications are anonymous and one-way. The person notifying you cannot see your details. You cannot see theirs.

We do not sell data. We do not build profiles. The system does one thing.
